# Privacy Policy — Open Turnitin

**Effective Date:** 2026  
**Last Updated:** 2026

At **Open Turnitin** ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our academic AI humanizer and citation platform handles your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Manuscript & Document Content
- **Zero Permanent Storage**: All text, documents (`.docx`, `.txt`, `.pdf`), citations, and drafts submitted to Open Turnitin are processed **strictly in-memory** during your active browser session.
- We **do not store, log, or index** your submitted manuscripts, papers, or generated outputs on any database or external server.

### B. Session Data
- **Local Execution**: All processing is local and ephemeral. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not share, sell, or monetize any user data with third parties or telemetry providers.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your manuscript content is deleted immediately upon completion of the transformation request.
